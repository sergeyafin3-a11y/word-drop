Lesson 2

Friends and drama

60 min

Four friends argue, stop talking and make up again in every season. These are the words for exactly that — and they work for real life too.

She always helps me.
a best friendto be good friendsto be upset to be angryto say sorryto be friends again to keep a secretto tell the truth
Task 1. Drag the lines by the ⠿ handle into the right order, then tell the story out loud in five sentences.

Six ways to give advice

You shouldthe normal one, friend to friend
You should talk to her today. · You shouldn’t wait.
Why don’t you…?softer — an idea, not an order
Why don’t you text him first?
I’d just…what I would do in your place
I’d just send one message.
I’d recommend…polite and a bit more serious · + verb -ing
I’d recommend talking to her face to face.
Have you tried…?when the person already did something · + verb -ing
Have you tried calling her?
If I were you…the strongest one — keep it for the end
If I were you, I would say sorry first.
— He never texts first. — Why don’t you text him today? If I were you, I would stop counting.

advice · a recommendation · a tip

advicewhat to do about a problem. One form for everything — never “advices”, never “an advice”.
Can you give me some advice? · One piece of advice: talk to her.
a recommendationwhat to choose: a film, a café, a book. You can count them.
Any recommendations for a series? · I have two recommendations.
a tipone small trick that works. You can count them too.
Here is a tip: text her in the morning.
Problems take advice. Choices take recommendations. Tips are short and practical.

And what do you say back?

You like the advice

That’s a good idea.
I’ll try that, thanks.

You don’t

I don’t think that will work.
Maybe later. I’m not ready.
Homework · Give advice in ten seconds. Six situations below. Your friend (Role A) says the problem — you are Role B, you answer. For each situation do three things:
1. read Role A out loud, like a real person, not like a text;
2. give two pieces of advice, each in a different form from the table above;
3. finish with one sentence: which advice you would take yourself, and why.
Ten seconds of thinking, then speak. Record all six on your phone and send them to me.

1 · No answer

Role A: “My best friend didn’t text me back for three days. I know she read my messages.”

2 · The birthday

Role A: “I forgot my best friend’s birthday. He saw the other messages and he is really upset.”

3 · Always late

Role A: “My friend is always late. Yesterday I waited forty minutes in the rain.”

4 · The secret

Role A: “I told my friend one secret. Now the whole class knows it.”

5 · No time

Role A: “I have a test on Monday and I have little time. I can’t start.”

6 · Careful!

Role A: “I want to watch something this weekend. Any ideas?”
This one is not a problem — it is a choice. Give a recommendation, not advice.

Check yourself. Did you use Why don’t you…? at least once? And I’d recommend…? And If I were you… in the hardest situation? If every answer started with “You should”, do it again.
Lesson 3

What people do

60 min

Today we go one step deeper than “nice” and “funny”: what people do, how it makes you feel, and why one bad action does not make a bad person. Every rule has one short task right after it.

Eight million people, and every one of them did something today

Warm-up · 5 min

No new words yet. Just answer — full sentences, not one word.

Say it now. Three sentences about the last episode, each with a different verb from the line above: somebody did something good, somebody did something bad, somebody did something you still don’t understand.

2 · The rule in two fingers · 5 min

👉 the thing → -ing

Point at the film, the song, the lesson.
The film is boring.
This song is amazing.

👈 me → -ed

Point at yourself. This is how you feel.
I am bored.
I am excited.

Six pairs you need

boreThe lesson is boring. → I am bored.
interestThe book is interesting. → I am interested.
exciteThe concert is exciting. → I am excited.
surpriseThe ending is surprising. → I am surprised.
tireThe trip was tiring. → I was tired.
relaxThis music is relaxing. → I am relaxed.
Task 1 · Drag and say. Put the right word in every gap, then read each sentence out loud.

3 · The trap · 4 min

One letter changes everything

I am bored.Nothing interesting is happening to me. ✅ what you mean
I am boring.People are bored with me. 😅 careful
after I am / he isabout a person → -ed, almost always

3After the trip I was very tiring.
4Are you interesting in football?

4 · so and such · 5 min

Which one?

so + adjectiveIt’s so good. · She was so tired.
such + a + adjective + nounIt’s such a good song.
the testIs there a noun after the adjective? Yes → such a. No → so.
Task 3 · so or such? Say the full sentence, don’t just say the word.
1This song is ___ good.
2It’s ___ a good song.
3The last episode was ___ sad.
4That was ___ a bad day.
1 so · 2 such · 3 so · 4 such

5 · One action is not the whole person · 6 min

What they did · who they are

the actionpast, one moment: She lied to me yesterday. · That was rude.
the personpresent, always: She is a liar. · He is a rude person.
the safe wayIt wasn’t like her. · He doesn’t usually do that.
why it mattersSay what they did, not what they are — and the conversation stays open.
Also useful: on purpose (he knew) against by mistake (he didn’t).
Task 4 · Change the label. I give you a label — you turn it into one action. “He is selfish” → “He didn’t help his friend when she asked.”
She is a bad friend.
He is rude.
She is annoying.
He is a liar.
They are selfish.

6 · The debate · 15 min

How to argue and stay friends

say your sideI think… · For me… · Honestly, I don’t think so.
give the reason…because… · For example, in the last episode…
half agreeMaybe, but… · I see what you mean, but…
come backAnd what about…? · So why did she…?
One rule: never stop after “I think so”. The next word is always because.
Task 5 · Pick a side. I read a sentence. You agree or you don’t, and you give one real example — from the show or from your own life. Then I take the other side and you answer me. Two rounds each, no one-word answers.
A small lie is fine if it protects your friend.
If somebody lies to you once, you can never trust them again.
A real friend tells you the truth, even when it hurts.
People who talk behind your back don’t change.
Saying sorry is easy. Doing something about it is the hard part.
You should always give a friend a second chance.

7 · One minute, no stopping · 5 min

Task 6 · The wrong thing. Start the timer and talk for 60 seconds: think of a person in the show who did something wrong. What exactly did they do? Was it on purpose or by mistake? Was it a bad action or a bad person? And what would you do in their place?
Three verbs from today and one so or such. I don’t correct you until the timer stops.
