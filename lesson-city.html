Six ways to give advice

You shouldthe normal one, friend to friend
You should talk to her today. · You shouldn’t wait.
Why don’t you…?softer — an idea, not an order
Why don’t you text him first?
I’d just…what I would do in your place
I’d just send one message.
I’d recommend…polite and a bit more serious · + verb -ing
I’d recommend talking to her face to face.
Have you tried…?when the person already did something · + verb -ing
Have you tried calling her?
If I were you…the strongest one — keep it for the end
If I were you, I would say sorry first.
— He never texts first. — Why don’t you text him today? If I were you, I would stop counting.

advice · a recommendation · a tip

advicewhat to do about a problem. One form for everything — never “advices”, never “an advice”.
Can you give me some advice? · One piece of advice: talk to her.
a recommendationwhat to choose: a film, a café, a book. You can count them.
Any recommendations for a series? · I have two recommendations.
a tipone small trick that works. You can count them too.
Here is a tip: text her in the morning.
Problems take advice. Choices take recommendations. Tips are short and practical.

And what do you say back?

You like the advice

That’s a good idea.
I’ll try that, thanks.

You don’t

I don’t think that will work.
Maybe later. I’m not ready.

2 · The rule in two fingers · 5 min

👉 the thing → -ing

Point at it: the film, the song, the lesson. The thing makes the feeling.

This song is amazing.

👈 me → -ed

Point at yourself. You get the feeling. People only — chairs are never tired.
I am bored.
I am excited.

1 · The new rule: so and such

so, or such a?

so + adjectiveIt’s so good. · She was so tired.
such a + adjective + nounIt’s such a good song. · He is such a kind person.
the testIs there a noun after the adjective? Yes → such a. No → so.
no “a” hereWith plural or uncountable: such good friends · such bad news.
Short cut: so + one word, such a + two words.

Что это вообще такое — по-русски

И so, и such переводятся одинаково — «такой, настолько». Они нужны, чтобы усилить признак: не просто «хороший», а «такой хороший». Разница только в том, что стоит дальше в предложении.

so — дальше только признак и всё. The song is so good. — песня такая хорошая. После good ничего нет, предложение кончилось.

such a — дальше признак и предмет. It’s such a good song. — это такая хорошая песня. После good стоит song, поэтому нужен such и артикль a.

Как выбрать за одну секунду. Посмотри, есть ли после прилагательного существительное (предмет, человек, день, песня). Есть — such a. Нет — so.

Когда «a» не нужна. Если предмет во множественном числе или его нельзя посчитать: such good friends (друзья), such bad news (новости). Артикль a бывает только с одним предметом.

3 · The four tenses, one more time

One table, four times

Present Simplealways, usually, every week — normal life
She always says sorry first.
Present Continuousright now, at the moment — look at it happening
Look, she is texting him now.
Past Simpleyesterday, last week, two days ago — finished
They had a big argument last week.
Future Simpletomorrow, tonight, next week — a plan or a promise
I will text her tonight.
The time word tells you the tense. Hear the time word first, then choose.
