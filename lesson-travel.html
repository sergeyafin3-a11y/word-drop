Travel words in real situations

15 min

Do not translate first. Read the mini-situation, guess the meaning, then tap Meaning.

check-in desk
You show your passport here and get your boarding pass.
“Where is the check-in desk for flight 217?”
The airport counter where you register for your flight.
boarding pass
It has your name, flight, seat and gate.
“Here is my boarding pass.”
A document or phone screen that lets you board the plane.
gate
You wait here before you get on the plane.
“Has the gate changed?”
The numbered airport area where passengers board.
carry-on bag
Ivan wants to take this small bag into the cabin.
“Can I take this as carry-on?”
A small bag you take with you onto the plane.
checked baggage
This suitcase travels under the plane, not with your seat.
“I have one bag to check in.”
A suitcase you give to the airline before security.
baggage claim
After landing, you go here to collect your suitcase.
“Where is baggage claim?”
The airport area where you pick up checked bags.
security
You put your bag on a belt and walk through a scanner.
“Do I need to take my laptop out?”
The airport safety check before the departure area.
delayed
The flight was planned for 15:00. Now it leaves at 17:20.
“How long is the delay?”
Later than the planned time.
aisle / window seat
Olga wants to look outside. Ivan wants to stand up easily.
“Could I have a window seat, please?”
Window = next to the window. Aisle = next to the walkway.

Hotel English you can actually use

12 min
reservation
You booked the room before you arrived.
“I have a reservation under Kovalev.”
check in / check out
Start your stay / finish your stay.
“What time is check-out?”
key card
A plastic card that opens your hotel room.
“My key card isn’t working.”
included
You do not pay extra for it.
“Is breakfast included?”
air conditioning
It cools the room when the weather is hot.
“The air conditioning doesn’t work.”
available
Free and ready to use.
“Is a family room available tonight?”
Make it personal: Choose three phrases you would really use on your next trip. Say each one with your own detail.

Lesson 3

The Big Travel Story

60 min

The story is original and written for A1–A2 practice. It uses the target travel vocabulary and all four target tenses.

Read: “Five Tickets, Four Passports and One Mystery Bag”

18 min
Part 1 — The plan

Zhenya wants to travel abroad before school starts. She finds cheap tickets to a small seaside city called Sunny Bay. “We can go for five days,” she says. Olga loves the idea and immediately makes a plan. Yuri chooses two museums and a historical walking tour. Valentina starts thinking about sandwiches for the journey. Ivan says they do not need a plan because “a good traveller can solve any problem.” Nobody agrees with him.

On Friday morning, they are getting ready to leave. Olga is checking the passports. Yuri is reading about the city. Zhenya is charging her phone. Valentina is putting food into a bag. Ivan is standing near the door and saying, “Everybody is too slow!”

Part 2 — The airport

At the airport, Olga goes to the check-in deskthe counter where you register for a flight. The agent asks for five passports. Olga gives her four.

“Four?” the agent asks.

Everyone looks at Ivan.

Ivan checks his jacket, his bag and his pockets. No passport. Valentina looks at him in silence. Zhenya says, “Please tell me it is in another pocket.”

Then Ivan smiles and takes the passport out of his cap. “Safe place,” he says. Olga does not smile.

They get their boarding passesdocuments that show your flight, gate and seat, but there is another problem: Valentina’s suitcase is too heavy. She opens it and takes out a large box of homemade food. “We need this,” she says. After a long discussion, the family moves some things into Ivan’s bag.

Part 3 — Arrival

The flight is delayed, but they finally land in Sunny Bay. At baggage claimthe place where you collect checked suitcases, everybody gets a suitcase except Ivan.

“My bag is missing,” he tells an airport worker.

The worker asks for his baggage receipt. Ivan cannot find it. Olga has it, of course.

Twenty minutes later, they discover the truth: Ivan took the wrong suitcase. His real bag is still on the belt. The wrong suitcase is pink and has a large sticker: “DANCE TEAM.” Zhenya takes a photo before Ivan can stop her.

Part 4 — The hotel

At the hotel, Olga says, “I have a reservation under Kovalev.” The receptionist looks at the computer and says there are only two rooms, not three. Olga shows the confirmation email. Yuri politely asks the receptionist to check again. Ivan wants to solve the problem “faster” and starts speaking very loudly.

Finally, the receptionist finds the third room. But now Ivan’s key card does not work. Valentina says this is because he laughed at everybody all day.

In the evening, they go out for dinner. Zhenya uses a map on her phone. Ivan says he remembers the way. Ten minutes later, they are standing in front of a closed pet shop.

Part 5 — Tomorrow

At dinner, they make a new plan. Tomorrow they will visit the old town. Yuri will take them to the museum. Olga will choose a good café. Valentina will try a local dish. Zhenya will make a travel video. Ivan says he will not use a map because maps “make people weak.”

The next morning, everybody is ready at 9:00. Everybody except Ivan.

His room is empty. His phone is on the table. On the bed there is a note:

“Don’t worry. I found something interesting. Meet me at the old station at 10. Bring the blue bag. Very important.”

Olga looks at the clock. The old station is on the other side of the city. Valentina looks at the blue bag. Zhenya opens it — and suddenly says, “Oh no. We have a problem.”

What is inside the blue bag? Where is Ivan? What happens next?
